Everything it does for you.

Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn, run from a chat with Claude or ChatGPT.

Instagram

  • Schedules reels, posts, carousels and stories
  • Sets custom reel covers
  • Converts videos to Instagram's best quality
  • Replies to comments with your keyword
  • DMs the link to everyone who comments
  • Checks they follow you before sending it
  • Follows up an hour later
  • Asks how it was and logs the answer
  • Answers story replies and mentions
  • Tracks who opened the link

YouTube

  • Uploads and schedules videos and Shorts
  • Puts your link in the description
  • Replies to keyword comments
  • Tracks views, likes and watch time

LinkedIn

  • Schedules text posts
  • Schedules image posts
  • Schedules videos
  • Schedules PDF carousels

3Across all three

  • One content calendar
  • Turns PDFs into flipbook links
  • Shrinks PDFs before sending
  • Emails you if a post fails
  • Sends a daily summary
  • Stays inside each app's limits
  • Keeps your logins active
Free and open source. Runs on Cloudflare's free tier.
